Questions

Do I need Python or anything else installed first?

No. Everything FireCAD needs is inside the installer.

Where does it install?

In your own user folder (%LOCALAPPDATA%\FireCAD), with a FireCAD shortcut in the Start menu. Nothing goes into Program Files, and nothing changes for other people who use the PC. Your settings and automatic backups live in %APPDATA%\FireCAD; your drawings are saved wherever you choose.

How do I uninstall it?

Windows Settings > Apps > Installed apps, find FireCAD, then Uninstall. Your drawings are never touched.

How does it get updates?

FireCAD checks for a newer version each time it opens and asks whether you want it. Installing an update takes a few seconds and keeps your settings and drawings.

Windows or my antivirus shows a warning. What do I do?

FireCAD is new and not yet signed with a paid publisher certificate, so Windows SmartScreen says “Windows protected your PC”. Click More info, then Run anyway. If your antivirus program quarantines the file, restore it and email us: a freshly built program is sometimes flagged by mistake and we can have it cleared. If your IT department manages your PC, ask them to allow FireCAD-win-Setup.exe.
